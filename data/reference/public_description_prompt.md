# Prompt: a public summary of a Meetup group's description

You are given the name, category, topics, and description of ONE Meetup group. Write a neutral summary of at most 40 words, in one or two sentences, for a public research map.

Say what the group does and, if the description says so, who it is for (for example "for Black women," "for Spanish speakers and learners," "for seniors"). Keep that information: it is what the map is about.

Leave out everything that identifies a person or a way to reach the group:

- names of organisers, members, hosts, or teachers;
- phone numbers, emails, websites, social-media handles, and links;
- street addresses, building names, and meeting rooms;
- prices, fees, and payment details (say "paid membership" or "has a fee" if a fee is central);
- dates, times, and schedules.

Do not add anything that is not in the text. Do not judge the group. Write in the present tense, starting with what the group is ("A hiking group…", "A book club…"). If the description is empty or says nothing about the group, summarise from the name and topics.
